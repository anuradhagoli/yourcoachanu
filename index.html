A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Leadership experience.
A coaching mindset.

Hello, I'm Anuradha — an ICF Associate Certified Coach and experienced technology leader. I believe leadership grows when people have the space to reflect, learn and choose how they want to show up.

Across more than 20 years in technology, engineering and business environments, I've led teams, supported transformation and worked alongside people navigating complexity, change and growth. My experience spans global organisations including Salesforce, Optum, Marsh McLennan and Novartis.

Alongside my leadership career, I have developed a professional coaching practice grounded in listening, curiosity and thoughtful challenge. My intention is to help leaders pause, think differently, recognise their strengths and take purposeful action.
